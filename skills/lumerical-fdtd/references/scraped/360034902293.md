> Understanding coherence in FDTD simulations FDTD FDTD is a fundamentally coherent simulation method. Below, we discuss three types of incoherence that are relevant for FDTD simulations, and the difficulties in direct simulation of these effects. In many physical processes, we have a combination of all types of incoherence. The following sections provide three examples that show how to model various incoherent effects with FDTD and MODE' propagator. Temporal phase incoherence The phase, j, of the light shifts randomly over time, on a time scale tc. For light near visible wavelengths, we have $$ \vec{E}(t)=\vec{E}_{0} \cos (\omega t+\varphi(t)) $$ $$ T=\frac{2 \pi}{\omega} \approx 10^{-15} S $$ $$ \tau_{c} \approx 10^{-11} s $$ It is not practical to simulate the statistics of temporal phase incoherence because tc >> T.
