Bases: FieldProjectionDetectorBase

Frequency-domain detector for projecting a phasor plane to observation angles.

theta and phi follow the global spherical-coordinate convention: theta is measured from the positive z-axis and phi from the positive x-axis in the x-y plane.

Subsampling stride for the phasor DFT. Only every stride-th active time step is recorded, with the kept samples rescaled to match every-step recording.
